Before you pay for any land

Whoever you buy from, check these four things. We walk you through each one for every property we sell.

Title documentsAsk for the Certificate of Occupancy or allocation papers and confirm them at the land registry.
Survey planPlot size and boundaries should match a registered survey.
Site visitWalk the land before you pay. Photos are not proof.
Written receipt and agreementEvery payment in writing, to a company account.
